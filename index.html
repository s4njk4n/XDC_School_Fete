🎡 Run your school fete on phones

The ticket system your fete already uses — buy tickets at the booth, spend them at the stalls — but on phones. Free for any school. Families don't need to download anything.

How it works

  1. Families buy tickets at the ticket booth with cash or card, just like now. The booth helper scans the family's phone and the tickets appear on it.
  2. They spend them at stalls. The stall shows a code; the family scans it and taps Pay. Both phones show a big green tick.
  3. At the end, you get the totals — every booth's cash and card, every stall's takings — ready for the treasurer.

What you'll need

  • One phone for the organiser (a laptop works too).
  • Your helpers' own phones — one per ticket booth and one per stall.
  • A few dollars' worth of XDC for "running credit" (the app explains how to get it). Not needed for a practice run.
  • A printer for the posters (or just show them on a phone).
  • About an hour to set up, plus a quick practice with your committee.

Setting up, step by step

The Organiser app walks you through all of this with a checklist — this is just so you know what's ahead.

  1. Do a practice run.
    Tap Try a free practice fete. It uses pretend credit, so your committee can try every step for free — selling, paying, refunds and totals.
  2. Set up the organiser phone.
    Open the Organiser app, add it to your home screen, and text yourself the backup when asked. That one text message lets you recover everything if your phone is lost.
  3. Add running credit.
    The app shows an address to send XDC to and how much you need (around 100 XDC for 500 families — usually a few dollars). Leftover credit stays for next year.
  4. Create your tickets.
    Enter your fete's name and the price of one ticket. Tap Create tickets. Then save your backup again — it now includes your fete.
  5. Invite your helpers.
    Tap Invite helpers to text each ticket booth and stall helper their link, or print the helper sheets. Each link opens the right app, already connected to your fete.
  6. Add each booth and stall.
    Each helper shows you a code in their app. Tap Add a booth or stall and scan it — that loads the booth with tickets and gives everyone a little running credit.
  7. Put up the family posters.
    Print the Pay with your phone! poster for the entrance and the ticket booth. Families scan it with their camera and they're in.
  8. On the day.
    The app has an "On the day" page with what to check before the gates open and how to handle common questions.
  9. Afterwards.
    Booths open Cash check to match the tin and card machine. You open Totals and download the spreadsheet.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

The apps are free. The only cost is a small amount of XDC "running credit" that pays the tiny network fees each time tickets move — usually a few dollars for a whole fete. Practice runs are completely free.

Do families have to install an app?

No. They scan the poster and it opens in their phone's browser. Adding it to the home screen is optional. Helpers are asked to add their app to the home screen because they'll use it all day.

What if someone loses their phone or their tickets disappear?

When anyone starts, the app asks them to text themselves a backup message. Pasting that message back into the app — on any phone — brings their tickets back. The same goes for the organiser, whose backup also brings back the whole fete and team list.

Do tickets have any value outside the fete?

No. Just like paper tickets, they're only for spending at your fete. The money itself never goes through the app — families pay the ticket booth with cash or card as usual.

What if the internet drops out?

Phones need mobile data or Wi-Fi to buy and spend tickets. The apps reconnect by themselves and show a notice while offline. We recommend keeping a roll of paper tickets at the booth as a backup, just in case.

How does it work behind the scenes?

Tickets are recorded on the XDC Network, a fast, low-cost public ledger, which is why every movement can be counted exactly for the totals. Nobody at the fete needs to know or do anything about that — it just works in the background.

Can families just use their phone's camera?

Yes. The poster and every stall's payment code work with the phone's ordinary camera — it opens straight to the right screen. The in-app Pay at a stall button works too.

Can lots of schools use this at the same time?

Yes. Each school creates its own tickets, so fetes are completely separate — one school's tickets can't be spent at another school's fete, and each school sees only its own totals. A family who goes to two fetes keeps the two sets of tickets apart automatically and can switch between them in Settings.

Can someone help us set it up?

Yes. Fete Tickets is built by XDC Outpost, and we're happy to help your school with setup, a practice run with your committee, running credit, or anything on the day. Get in touch via xdcoutpost.xyz.

Can we use it again next year?

Yes. In the Organiser app, go to Settings → Set up a different fete. Any leftover running credit carries over.
