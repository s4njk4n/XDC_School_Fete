      On the day

      Before gates open

      1. Every booth and stall phone opens its app and shows a balance (refresh on your dashboard to check).
      2. Ticket booths have tickets loaded; everyone has running credit (no warnings on your dashboard).
      3. Families' posters are up at the entrance and the ticket booth.
      4. Phones are charged — bring a power bank or two for booths.

      During the fete

      • A family can't pay — "needs a top-up"Ticket booth taps Fix a phone and scans them. Free, takes seconds.
      • A booth is running low on ticketsTap the booth in Your team → Load more tickets.
      • Someone's tickets disappearedThey open the app, tap I've used this before, and paste their backup text.
      • A family wants their money backTicket booth taps Buy back; the app says how much cash to hand over.
      • No phone / no internetKeep a roll of paper tickets at the booth as a backup.

      After the fete

      1. Each booth opens Cash check — cash and card totals to match against the tin and the card machine.
      2. Booths send unsold tickets back; stalls hand in their tickets.
      3. You open Totals and download the spreadsheet for the treasurer.
